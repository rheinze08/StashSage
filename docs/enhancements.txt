Market price is not a thing, we do not and will never have data on completed transactions or trades. It's just a sea of offer data. Hence the challenge. I personally do not think the trade system will change, as you cannot design a more efficient trade system around the way itemization works. You can't just do a WoW-style auction house, fixed timers will not work. It's all about price discovery.

You can never know if you sold something for less than it's "worth" (since that's not well-defined), but on the positive side, you save time and increase your liquidity by immediately booking the trade, even if you underpriced it.

The challenges you mentioned are exactly why I developed my app, StashSage, to cut through the noise, to try to define similarity/correlation based on ar/es/ev/mods to predict a prevailing offer price to start your trades at. Also it gives you an opportunity to passively collect more demand data (trade offers being pinged to you) so that you can know if you are underpricing something, because not all offers will arrive instantaneously and sometimes the "price discovery" mechanism takes time to play out, precisely because people might think your offers are fake or that you won't sell. It's just a big market, but my personal goal is to increase speed and efficiency and therefore liquidity in the long-run.
